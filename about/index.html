About the blog

Technology explained through real building.

Nnamdi's Dev Notes documents practical lessons from software projects, experiments, mistakes, and conversations.

Why this site exists

Learning software development can feel overwhelming when every explanation assumes too much background knowledge. This blog aims to slow things down, explain the important decisions, and connect each concept to something useful.

Tutorials combine video and written notes, podcast pages preserve the best ideas and resources from each conversation, and articles make complex topics easier to revisit.

Who is behind it?

I am Nnamdi Ileh, a software engineer who enjoys turning ideas into dependable products. I write from the perspective of someone who is still learning, building, reviewing decisions, and sharing the useful parts with others.

Good technical content should help you understand what to do, why it works, and what could go wrong.

What you can expect

The focus is practical web development, JavaScript, backend engineering, accessibility, product thinking, and the professional skills developers need to work well with other people.

Editorial principles

How the content is made

1

Practical first

Every lesson should connect to a real development task or decision.

2

Clear, not clever

Simple explanations are preferred over unnecessary jargon and abstraction.

3

Honest trade-offs

There is rarely one perfect solution, so limitations and alternatives are discussed.
